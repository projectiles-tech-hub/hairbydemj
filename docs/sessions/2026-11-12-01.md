# Session 2026-11-12-01

## What was done

- Module 8 (Shipping) built on `feature/shipping`, on top of modules 2 to 7:
  packing the bag into the store's boxes; zones and prices inside Nigeria;
  DHL Express (MyDHL API), UPS and FedEx clients over REST for prices,
  labels and tracking; conversion of carrier prices to the customer's
  currency with markup; 30-minute quotes that checkout must use; the
  duties notice; labels kept in a private folder; tracking that moves the
  order to shipped and delivered; local courier dispatch and delivery.
- After a payment is confirmed, carrier orders get a `buy_label` job
  queued for the worker (module 10).
- Checks: 184 tests pass against PostgreSQL 16, with stand-in carriers.

## Decisions made

- Inside Nigeria only the staff zones are offered; carriers are for
  addresses abroad.
- Carrier prices are rounded up to each currency's rounding.
- A free-delivery discount makes the cheapest option free.
- What a label costs us is kept with the quote and shipment, never shown to
  customers.
- UPS labels come as GIF images (UPS does not offer PDF); DHL and FedEx as
  4 x 6 inch PDFs.
