## Decisions made

- The order is written at checkout, waiting for payment with its stock held,
  so the payment module has an order to charge; it becomes paid only when
  the payment is confirmed.
- Gift cards pay before the card and can cover delivery.
- Return refunds go to the original payment first, then to gift cards used.
  Delivery is refunded only when staff choose, once at most.
- Final-sale items can come back only for a fault.
- Sample figures (14-day window, 10% store credit bonus) apply until the
  owner sets them; they are store settings staff can change.
