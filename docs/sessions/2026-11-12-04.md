## Decisions made

- Our code signs with DKIM and hands mail to Postfix on the server, which
  handles delivery and retries; Postfix listens only on the machine itself.
- Order confirmations, cancellations, returns and store credit always go;
  order progress emails and push follow the customer's choices; offers need
  marketing consent and stop for good on unsubscribe.
- WhatsApp messages stay dropped (decision 0004); the template table's
  WhatsApp switches are not used.
