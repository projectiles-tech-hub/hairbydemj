## What was done

- Module 12 (Search) built on `feature/search`, on top of modules 2 to 11:
  query tidying, lengths from the words typed, typo correction against the
  words the shop knows (kept up to date by database triggers), finishing the
  last word, matching on everything a product can be found by, "any word"
  when nothing has all, ranking, search inside the shop product list (all
  filters, sorts and pages shared), suggestions, and the staff report.
- Checks: 228 tests pass against PostgreSQL 16.

## Decisions made

- Search lives inside the existing product list address (`?q=`), so the
  website and app need no separate search page logic for filters.
- Typo correction uses trigram similarity of at least 0.3 against known
  words; everyday words are dropped rather than corrected.
- Search records keep only the words and the count found, never who
  searched, and are removed after 180 days.
